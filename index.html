Hydrogen fuel cell engine

Follow hydrogen from the tank to the motor. Drag to orbit, pinch or scroll to zoom, or press Play tour.

Hydrogen H₂ Protons H⁺ Electrons e⁻ Oxygen O₂ Water H₂O Membrane

Anode

H₂ → 2H⁺ + 2e⁻

A platinum catalyst splits each hydrogen molecule into protons and electrons.

Membrane

H⁺ pass, e⁻ blocked

Only protons cross the polymer layer, so the electrons must take the outside route through the motor.

Cathode

½O₂ + 2H⁺ + 2e⁻ → H₂O

Oxygen from compressed air meets the protons and electrons and forms water, plus some heat.

Stack

5 cells shown, in series

Each cell makes about 0.7 V. Real stacks chain hundreds of cells to reach motor voltage.
